About Our Church

Divine Power Of Christ Ministries is a non-denominational church of Christ based on the foundation of holiness and Righteousness.

The ministry's vision is to reflect God's power through the teachings of Christ. Founded in 2008, we have been a beacon of hope and faith in our city for over 18 years.

Our mission is to lead people into a growing relationship with Jesus Christ through worship, discipleship, and service. We believe in the power of prayer, the importance of community, and the transformative work of the Holy Spirit.

Whether you're new to faith or have been walking with God for years, you'll find a welcoming community at Divine Ministries where you can grow spiritually and connect with others on the same journey.

We are evidently living in the Last days and witnessing broad opening of scroll of Matthew 24 where Apocalyptic events mentioned by Jesus Christ are being revealed before our very eyes.

People of God, the church has generally fallen to slumber and allowed every worldliness inside it and its time for us to remind all and sound alarm to the church that the Journey to the promised land is still on. A call to maturity, true gospel and salvation

I greet you all in the precious name of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. We are living in a very critical generation—a generation with much knowledge, much activity, and many churches, yet also a generation facing great spiritual confusion...

A Message for Today’s Church Greetings in the precious name of our Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ.

We thank God for the gift of life and the opportunity to gather together as His people. We find ourselves in a pivotal moment—a generation filled with knowledge, activity, and many churches, yet also marked by profound spiritual confusion.

As we reflect on our calling, let us turn to Scripture:

  • “And I tell you, you are Peter, and on this rock I will build my church, and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it.” (Matthew 16:18)
  • “For I am not ashamed of the gospel, for it is the power of God for salvation to everyone who believes…” (Romans 1:16)

    • Today, we are invited to honestly consider:

    1. The true state of the Church today
    2. The Gospel we are proclaiming
    3. The meaning of salvation in our generation

    The State of the Church Today

    The Church has expanded in numbers, buildings, and programs. Yet true growth is measured not merely by attendance, but by spiritual maturity. While congregations and activities have multiplied, we must ask: Are we growing deeper in Christ, or only wider in influence?

    We are called to be salt and light—yet too often, the world shapes us more than we shape the world. Still, we give thanks to God, who has preserved a faithful remnant—men and women who continue to hunger for righteousness and truth.

    The Gospel in This Generation

    The Gospel is not a message of mere prosperity, popularity, or comfort. It is the good news that Jesus Christ came, died for our sins, was buried, and rose again to save humanity (1 Corinthians 15:1–4).

    Today, there is a danger of preaching a gospel that draws crowds but does not transform hearts. The true Gospel convicts, calls for repentance, and leads to a life unmistakably changed by grace.

    May we be a Church grounded in truth, powered by the Gospel, and alive in genuine salvation.

    — Church Administrator
