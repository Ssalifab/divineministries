About Our Church

Divine Power Of Christ Ministries is a non-denominational church of Christ based on the foundation of holiness and Righteousness.

The ministry's vision is to reflect God's power through the teachings of Christ. Founded in 2008, we have been a beacon of hope and faith in our city for over 18 years.

Our mission is to lead people into a growing relationship with Jesus Christ through worship, discipleship, and service. We believe in the power of prayer, the importance of community, and the transformative work of the Holy Spirit.

Whether you're new to faith or have been walking with God for years, you'll find a welcoming community at Divine Ministries where you can grow spiritually and connect with others on the same journey.

Head of Ministry
Apostle Ambrose Twesigye

Message to the Church and the World

Our message to the Church and the World is basically preparation! We are evidently living in the Last days and witnessing broad opening of scroll of Matthew 24 where Apocalyptic events mentioned by Jesus Christ are being revealed before our very eyes.

Preparation for the last days: We are living in the Last days and must remain vigilant.

The Lord Jesus emphasized in Matthew 25 that the church has two categories: wise virgins who remain vigilant and foolish virgins who have lost their oil.

We are called to raise Teachers, Pastors, Evangelists, Prophets and Apostles and send them out to communities to prepare the church for the soon return of our Lord Jesus Christ.

— Apostle Ambrose Twesigye

Head of Music

Welcome to Divine Power of Christ Ministries! As the Music Director, I am delighted to serve and lead our congregation in worship through inspiring music and heartfelt praise...

Our music ministry is dedicated to creating an atmosphere where everyone can encounter God's presence, experience spiritual renewal, and grow deeper in faith.

We invite you to join us in lifting our voices and hearts in praise.

Administrator
Church Admin

A call to maturity, true gospel and salvation

I greet you all in the precious name of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. We are living in a very critical generation—a generation with much knowledge, much activity, and many churches, yet also a generation facing great spiritual confusion...

Reflection: the state of the Church, the Gospel we are preaching, and the meaning of salvation in our generation.

The Gospel: not prosperity alone, popularity, or comfort—Jesus Christ died and rose again to save mankind.

Salvation: not church membership—it's a personal relationship with Jesus Christ, marked by repentance, faith, and obedience.

— Church Administrator
